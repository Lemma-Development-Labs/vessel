# /product/ballast

## Ballast (levered underwriting seat)

Ballast is the underwriting seat sold as levered yield:

- It is the **first-loss** tranche.
- It earns the residual after Hull’s fixed coupon is satisfied.
- It is *not* insurance: Ballast losses are real accounting outcomes in the waterfall.

Subordination is enforced on-chain (it reverts if you try to breach the posted floor).
