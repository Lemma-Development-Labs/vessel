## vUSD / svUSD (testnet)

vUSD and svUSD are part of Vessel’s product surface (coming up on testnet):

- vUSD: the dollar minted against the verified book.
- svUSD: the staked variant.

In this repository snapshot, vUSD/svUSD are still treated as “coming up” on testnet: do not assume minting is live until a mint tx is shipped and recorded under `# Shipped hashes` in `docs/ADDRESSES.md`.
