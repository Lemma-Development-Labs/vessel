## Findings

- **Perpl mark on a fork.** The mark goes stale after 60 s and the engine (correctly) refuses to
  trade. A mark push by Perpl's owner (`updateMarkPricePNSByOwner`) alone reverts on the equally
  frozen external oracle. After `setIgnOracle(64, true)` the push is accepted and shorts fill. The
  rehearsal does both as the impersonated owner `0x582e…93B1`, on the fork only, and re-posts the
  current mark every 5 s. Anvil cannot reuse a block timestamp, so holding time still is not an option.
- **Kuru `FlipOrderCreated`** carries every field in the event data (nothing indexed), unlike
  docs.kuru.io. `KuruQuotes` reads both layouts.
- **Public RPC.** `testnet-rpc.monad.xyz` timed out while building forks today. `FORK_RPC` lets the
  rehearsal fork from another endpoint (Ankr worked).
- The keeper made 15 actions in 150 s on a 1,000 USDC book: slices are 0.5% of value, so a small book
  needs many slices to reach its target split.

## Kuru quoter rehearsed (2026-10-08)

`e2ba6c3` added the testnet Kuru quoter (`vessel-service/src/v2/quoter.ts`, `quoterLoop.ts`): the
seeder, never the keeper, keeps Vessel's two books quoted around the Perpl mark through the same
journal. Rehearsal (`--rehearse`, 200 s, MON moved +60 bps halfway):

| | |
|---|---|
| Safe batches v2h-01, v2h-03, **v2h-02 (Hull series 1)** | all executed; `activeSeries 1` |
| Quoter | 2 approvals, 3 margin deposits, cancelled the seeding script's flip orders on both books, posted both books, and after the price move re-centred both (`mid 59 bps from mark`) |
| Keeper | 23 confirmed actions; engine 814.11 MON spot vs 652-lot short mid-pair, 406.52 USDC still to deploy |
| `vessel-verify` | engine.backing, engine.value, hedge.gap PASS; OVERALL PASS |
| Refused (no gas spent) | `buySpot` once with Kuru `InsufficientLiquidity()`, in the moment the book was empty between cancelling the flip orders and posting; retried next tick |

Findings:
- **Kuru flip pairs:** a partly filled flip order spawns a linked order, and the two point at each
  other (`flippedId`). Cancelling one cancels both; naming both reverts the batch
  (`OrderAlreadyFilledOrCancelled`). The quoter and `KuruQuotes` name one per pair. Fork probe.
- **Kuru cancels are not idempotent**, contrary to its docs: a filled or gone id reverts the batch.
  `KuruQuotes` checks `s_orders` first; the quoter only cancels what it just read as live.
- **Monad RPC state window:** `Required data unavailable` means the node no longer serves the
  forked block's state. Long rehearsals fail when they touch new storage late; start them fresh.
