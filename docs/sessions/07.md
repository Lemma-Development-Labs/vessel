## How to run it

```bash
FORK_RPC=https://rpc.ankr.com/monad_testnet KEEPER_SECONDS=150 scripts/v2-deploy-hedged.sh --rehearse
```

`--rehearse` forks testnet with Anvil and never touches the real chain or `deployments/`. Without
the flag the same script broadcasts for real (see "Real run" below).

## Findings

- **Perpl mark on a fork.** The mark goes stale after 60 s and the engine (correctly) refuses to
  trade. A mark push by Perpl's owner (`updateMarkPricePNSByOwner`) alone reverts on the equally
  frozen external oracle. After `setIgnOracle(64, true)` the push is accepted and shorts fill. The
  rehearsal does both as the impersonated owner `0x582e…93B1`, on the fork only, and re-posts the
  current mark every 5 s. Anvil cannot reuse a block timestamp, so holding time still is not an option.
- **Kuru `FlipOrderCreated`** carries every field in the event data (nothing indexed), unlike
  docs.kuru.io. `KuruQuotes` reads both layouts.
- **Public RPC.** `testnet-rpc.monad.xyz` timed out while building forks today. `FORK_RPC` lets the
  rehearsal fork from another endpoint (Ankr worked).
- The keeper made 15 actions in 150 s on a 1,000 USDC book: slices are 0.5% of value, so a small book
  needs many slices to reach its target split.
