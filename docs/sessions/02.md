## Architecture decisions

- Composition: RequestQueue, HullSeries, ReserveLedger and BetaAdmission are
  internal to `TrancheController`; custody, escrow and Ballast units are
  separate contracts it deploys, so every link is immutable (no setters).
- Book kept in USDC base units (bit-identical to the reference model);
  18-decimal USD valuation arrives with the ValuationAdapter (S3).
- Hull units are non-transferable ledger entries; Ballast units a
  non-transferable ERC-20 view.
- Rounding: payouts never exceed entitlement; ≤ 1 base unit per holder per
  funding stays in the series escrow pool (documented, tested).
- Hull cancellations freeze at the **window end**, not at activation (bug
  found by a coverage test and fixed).
- Governance is an address today; the 2-of-3 Safe + 48 h timelock wiring
  lands with deployment (Day 2).
