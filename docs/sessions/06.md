## HedgedEngine (built the same day)

`contracts/src/core/engine/HedgedEngine.sol` implements `IStrategyEngine`:

- **Holds:** an internal ledger of USDC, AUSD and MON, plus its own Perpl
  account. Donations are quarantined.
- **Value:** cash + MON × price + (AUSD + Perpl equity) × FX. Perpl equity is
  free balance + position deposit + marked PnL, and PnL includes funding.
  A cash-only book is valued at the current block; anything priced carries the
  price's own timestamp, so the controller's 60 s check applies.
- **Keeper actions:** `buySpot`, `sellSpot`, `openShort`, `closeShort`,
  `convertToMargin`, `convertToCash`, `depositMargin`, `withdrawMargin`.
  Governance can run every reducing action, so a lost keeper key cannot strand
  a position.
- **On-chain bounds:**
  - slice ≤ min(100 USDC, 0.5% of value);
  - spot and short differ by at most one slice, unless a trade narrows the gap;
  - 30 bps spot and perp bands, 20 bps conversion floor, all computed on chain;
  - no trades on prices older than 60 s;
  - RISK_INCREASE pause stops buys and new shorts only.
- **Prices (testnet):** `src/core/testnet/PerplMarkValuation.sol` uses the
  Perpl mark and AUSD at par, says `isIndependent() == false`, and refuses
  chain 143. Mainnet needs a G03 feed.

Evidence: `test/core/HedgedEngine.t.sol` (31 pass, CI). `test/fork/HedgedEngineFork.t.sol`
(opt-in) runs the real core on Circle USDC against real Perpl and Vessel-opened
Kuru markets: 2,000 USDC admitted, 1,800 deployed, 600 converted to margin,
three paired slices (826.97 MON spot vs 826-lot short), settle, unwind,
1,799.16 USDC recalled. A pause blocks new risk while reductions still run.

Finding: Kuru's market-order `minOut` is `uint256` in the deployed bytecode;
docs.kuru.io says `uint96`, and that selector does not exist.
