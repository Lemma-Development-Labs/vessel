## Decisions (founder direction, 2026-10-05)

1. **One user dollar: Circle USDC.** dUSD and its faucet are retired at the
   next deployment; testers use faucet.circle.com (20 USDC / 2 h / address).
   The only other dollars are the deferred vUSD/svUSD lab (spec §18).
2. **AUSD is engine-internal.** Perpl accepts only AUSD as margin on both
   networks, so the engine converts USDC⇄AUSD through Kuru. Users never hold it.
3. **Vessel opens its own Kuru markets on testnet** (MON/USDC with Circle USDC,
   MON/AUSD with Perpl's AUSD), because Kuru's only testnet market quotes its own
   test "USD Coin" and had no bids. Mainnet uses the official Kuru markets.
4. **Fresh deployment** with a `HedgedEngine` (`setEngine` is once-only).

## Completed and how to use it

Fork proofs, opt-in so CI never touches the network:

```bash
cd contracts
VENUE_FORK=1 forge test --match-path 'test/fork/*Proof*' -vv \
  --fork-url https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz --fork-retries 8 \
  --fork-retry-backoff 1500 --compute-units-per-second 300
```

| Proof | Result |

## Findings

- The first Kuru seed (vault only) cost 6% on 100 USDC: the vault spreads
  liquidity across 1% price steps. Flip orders at ±3 bps fixed it with less capital.
- Fork tests cannot `deal` AUSD (non-standard storage); they take it from the
  Perpl exchange balance. Circle USDC is minted through its master minter.
- Live book at block 68,445,192: Ballast 100 dUSD, reserve 20 dUSD, engine
  102 dUSD, epoch 52, every `/v1/book` check PASS.
